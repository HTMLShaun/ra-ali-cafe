Our story

Ra Ali Café started with a simple idea: a cup of coffee should feel like it was made for you. Every drink is pulled and mixed to order, and every rice meal comes out of the kitchen the way you'd cook it at home — a little extra care, a little extra garlic rice. It's why regulars keep coming back, and why cyclists have made us their favorite rest stop after a long ride.
